Dance Class on Saturdays

Hi, I'm Javiera. Every Saturday afternoon, I go to a dance class in my neighborhood. It's a lot of fun, and I always meet interesting people there.


My classmate Ignacio comes with me every week. He's a nurse at the local hospital, but he loves music and dancing in his free time. He's from Argentina, and he's twenty-six years old.


Our dance teacher is Fernanda. She's from Peru, and she's very patient with beginners. She teaches salsa and bachata on Saturdays, and on Sundays she teaches yoga in the same studio.


My best friend Camila doesn't dance, but she always waits for me at a café near the studio. She works at a small bakery in the mornings, and she studies nursing in the evenings.


After class, we usually go to Camila's café and talk about our week. I love my Saturdays!

1. Who goes to a dance class every Saturday afternoon?
2. Who is from Argentina?
3. What is Ignacio's job?
4. Who teaches yoga on Sundays?
5. What does Camila do while Javiera is in her dance class?
6. What does Camila study in the evenings?
